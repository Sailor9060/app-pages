Privacy Policy

Harmonie2 for iOS/iPadOS · Last updated: July 2026

Summary

Harmonie2 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is gathered in the first place.

What Harmonie2 does

Harmonie2 is a MIDI effect plug-in (AUv3 MIDI FX) that processes MIDI note data entirely on your device, inside the host app you load it in (e.g. AUM). It transforms incoming MIDI notes into outgoing MIDI notes according to a configuration you write and keep on your device.

Data collection

Harmonie2 does not use the network. It does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or any other personal data category. The MIDI configuration you write is stored only locally, inside the host app's project/session data or the app's own local storage — never uploaded anywhere.

Third parties

Harmonie2 does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any.

Children's privacy

Harmonie2 does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, for the reasons above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
